Quran Prompter

Start reciting from anywhere in the Quran and the prompter finds your place, colors every word you recite, and scrolls the ayahs with you. When the surah ends or you fall silent, it goes back to listening — all without a sound or a vibration, and without touching the phone.

When is it useful?

  • Night prayer and Tahajjud — put the phone in front of you instead of holding a Mushaf, and recite at length with confidence.
  • Sunnah and voluntary prayers — recite what you have not memorized yet, without stopping to turn pages.
  • Following a sheikh's recitation — place the phone near the sound and the prompter follows ayah by ayah.
  • Memorization circles — the student recites; the teacher sees their place and their words on screen.
  • For anyone who needs very large text — large text on a black background, readable from a distance.
  • For anyone who does not read Arabic well — each word's pronunciation in Latin letters appears under it as you recite.

Privacy

Recognition runs entirely inside your browser: your audio is never sent to any server, and the site needs no account.
