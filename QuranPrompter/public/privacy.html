Privacy Policy

Quran Prompter · ملقّن القرآن — prompter.alketab.app · last updated 6 October 2026

This site collects nothing about you. Your voice never leaves your device.

Your voice

The site listens to your recitation through the microphone in order to follow your place in the Quran. That audio is processed entirely inside your browser, on your own device, by a speech model the site downloads once and keeps. It is never recorded, never stored, and never sent anywhere. It exists only in memory, for as long as it takes to match the next word, and is then discarded.

Leaving the reading screen, or switching to another tab, closes the microphone: the recording indicator goes out.

What the site downloads

  • From this site: the app itself, the text of the Quran, its fonts, and the 72 MB speech model — once, on your first visit. After that the site opens and works with no network.
  • A translation of the meanings, only if you choose one in the settings: one file from this site, kept on your device.
  • «More languages…», only if you open it: the list of translations, and the one you pick, come directly from QuranEnc.com, the publisher of those translations.
  • Tapping a word to hear it: that word's recitation is played from audio.qurancdn.com.

Like every website, the servers that deliver these files receive the ordinary details of each request, such as your IP address. The site itself adds no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

What is stored on your device

In your browser's storage for this site, readable by nothing else:

  • your settings: the interface language, text size, mode, highlight colours, background, Latin letters, mistake sound, and the translation you chose;
  • the last ayah you reached, so the site can offer to resume from it;
  • offline copies of the app, the speech model, and the translation you chose.

Clearing this site's data in your browser removes all of it.

What there is none of

  • No account, no sign-in, no email address.
  • No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifier.
  • No cookies and no tracking.
  • No data shared with anyone, because none is gathered.

Contacting us

«Contact us» on the About page opens an email in your own mail app, with technical details pre-filled — the app version, your browser, device and screen, your settings, and the last ayah you reached — to help us with a problem. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself, and you can edit or remove those details first.

Children

The site is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data at all, it collects none from children either.

Permissions the site asks for

  • Microphone — to hear your recitation and follow your place. Nothing else.

Changes

If the site's behaviour ever changes, this page changes before the release does, and the date above is updated.

ما يُنزّله الموقع

  • من هذا الموقع: التطبيق نفسه، ونص القرآن، وخطوطه، ونموذج التعرّف على الصوت (٧٢ ميغابايت) — مرة واحدة في أول زيارة، ثم يفتح الموقع ويعمل دون إنترنت.
  • ترجمة المعاني، إن اخترتها فقط من الإعدادات: ملف واحد من هذا الموقع يُحفَظ على جهازك.
  • «لغات أخرى…»، إن فتحتها فقط: قائمة الترجمات والترجمة التي تختارها تأتي مباشرة من موسوعة القرآن الكريم (QuranEnc.com) ناشرة هذه الترجمات.
  • لمس كلمة لسماعها: تُشغَّل تلاوة تلك الكلمة من audio.qurancdn.com.

وكأي موقع، تتلقّى الخوادم التي تقدّم هذه الملفات تفاصيل الطلب المعتادة، كعنوان IP. والموقع نفسه لا يضيف أي تحليلات ولا أي تتبّع.
